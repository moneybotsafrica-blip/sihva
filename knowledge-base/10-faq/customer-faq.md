# Customer frequently asked questions

## Can I check out without an account?

Yes. Checkout supports guest and signed-in customers.

## Why did the price, availability, or delivery selection change?

The server rechecks product status, options, stock, price, currency, coupon eligibility, and available delivery during checkout.

## Can I change my account email myself?

No. The profile flow allows name and phone updates but says email changes need ownership verification and are not available there.

## When will my review appear?

Reviews are submitted for moderation and become public only once published.

## How do I start a return?

Contact support within 14 days of delivery with your order number and reason. Review the return conditions first.
