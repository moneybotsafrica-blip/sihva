# Sidai Artistry product overview

## Overview

Sidai Artistry is an online commerce system for African luxury and handcrafted products. Customers can browse the catalogue, search, add products to a cart or wishlist, check out by card, manage an account, review products, and contact support. The public site also publishes artisan, story, journal, impact, shipping, returns, privacy, and terms pages.

## Main capabilities

- Browse active products, collections, categories, product images, and available options.
- Build a cart, apply an eligible coupon, select available delivery, and pay through Stripe Checkout.
- Register, sign in, recover a password, manage an address, and view eligible order history.
- Submit a product review for moderation and contact the support team.
